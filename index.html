Brain HQ

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use · YouTube Terms of Service · Google Privacy Policy

What it is

Brain HQ is a desktop program that runs on one personal Windows PC. Its owner uses it to schedule and upload videos he makes to his own YouTube channels (Slow Orbit, MountainBorn, Time Slipped and The Closer Files).

How it uses YouTube

  • It uses the YouTube Data API v3 (YouTube API Services) to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and the scheduled publish time, and to read the channel's own name and video list.
  • It only signs in to channels that belong to its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It does not download, scrape or repost anyone else's content, and it does not collect data about viewers.
