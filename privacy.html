Brain HQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Brain HQ is a private desktop tool run by Noah Ghanem (MountainBorn Productions, Calgary, Alberta, Canada). It is used only by its owner to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data it touches.

YouTube API Services

Brain HQ uses YouTube API Services. By using Brain HQ you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data Brain HQ accesses

  • Google sign-in tokens for the owner's own YouTube channels, created when the owner signs in with Google.
  • Channel information: the channel's name and ID, and the list, titles and status of the channel's own videos.
  • Upload data: the video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times that the owner creates and chooses to upload.

Brain HQ does not access, collect or store information about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos, and to check that an upload worked. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. They are never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • Brain HQ does not use cookies or tracking on this website or in the app.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept only while the channel is linked. Data from the YouTube API is not kept for more than 30 days unless it is refreshed. The owner can remove Brain HQ's access at any time from the Google security settings page, which makes the stored tokens stop working, and can delete the stored files from the computer at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
